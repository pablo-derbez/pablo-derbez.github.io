I am a PhD student in Economics at Indiana University Bloomington. My research focuses on inflation expectations in emerging economies and how beliefs shape economic behavior.

Before starting the PhD, I was an economist at Banco de México (2021–2025) in the Directorate General of Economic Research, where I analyzed inflation for the Bank’s Governing Board and helped design and test a new survey of consumer inflation expectations. I hold a BA in Economics from El Colegio de México.
